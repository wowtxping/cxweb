Cookie policy

Privacy policyTerms of useCookie policy

These policies are provided in English and Simplified Chinese. Please email us if you need help understanding them.

1. Our approach

Cookies are small browser records; localStorage is a different browser storage mechanism to which similar privacy rules can apply. This website keeps essential privacy choices and offers three optional features: remembered language, Tencent / Knocket chat and Google Analytics. They are off until allowed. We do not install advertising pixels. Hosting/security processing is described in the Privacy policy.

2. Essential privacy choices

spring-snow-privacy-v2 is first-party localStorage containing the policy version, three permission flags, choice source and saved/expiry timestamps. It is necessary to respect your selection, is not a visitor identifier, and expires after 180 days. It is saved when you make a choice; if browser storage is blocked, a choice can apply only to the current page.

3. Optional language preference

chunxue-language is first-party localStorage holding a language code. It is read and written only if you allow remembered language. Its effective lifetime is limited by the 180-day privacy choice, and it is removed when permission expires or is withdrawn. Without permission, you can still change the current language; language-specific page URLs carry that choice without browser storage.

4. Optional Google Analytics

With Analytics permission, Google Analytics 4 uses first-party _ga and _ga_* cookies to measure visits and contact-button clicks. Cookie lifetime is set to at most 180 days without automatic renewal. These identifiers may constitute personal data. Google may process IP/device information outside the EEA. Advertising features are disabled. Rejecting Analytics blocks its tag entirely, including cookieless consent-mode requests. Withdrawing permission removes accessible Analytics cookies and reloads an active tag. Server-side Google retention follows the actual property settings.

5. Optional chat storage and requests

Tencent / Knocket is loaded only after chat permission and activation. The SDK may store visitor/session data, authentication or message cache and service state, including keys starting with knocket:, TIM_, visitorId or imUserInfo. Names and retention can vary with the provider version and account settings. The provider may process IP/device data and service diagnostics. Our script-source restrictions block the separately identified trtc.io/js/shence814.min.js analytics loader; this is not a claim that all provider operational logging is disabled. See Tencent / Knocket privacy information.

6. Rejecting or withdrawing permission

Use to reject optional features or change each permission. Allowing chat does not automatically download it on every page; it still waits for activation. If you turn off chat after the SDK has loaded, the page reloads to stop it and removes identifiable chat storage/cookies accessible to this site. We cannot delete HttpOnly or third-party-domain cookies or provider-held messages from this page. Your browser can clear site data, and info@sscakh.com can receive requests about delivered enquiries.

7. Hosting, external sites and changes

Cloudflare provides hosting/security and may use provider-managed security cookies when its protections require them; availability and lifetime depend on the protection actually used. See the Cloudflare privacy policy. We do not embed Google Maps: a selected address link opens its external page. External sites control their own storage. We update this policy when the site’s storage practices change and request a new choice when the consent version changes.
