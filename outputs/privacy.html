These policies are provided in English and Simplified Chinese. Please email us if you need help understanding them.

1. Who is responsible

Spring Snow Textile Co., Ltd. is the data controller for website enquiries and the website choices described here. The site presents the CHUN XUE and SSCA factories in Cambodia. Contact info@sscakh.com for privacy questions or requests; our factory contact addresses are listed in the footer. A factory named in your quotation or contract may separately handle order records.

2. Information and purposes

Browsing necessarily exposes connection information such as IP address, requested URL, browser details and request time to our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, to deliver and protect the site. If you contact us, we receive the contact details, employer or business information, messages and attachments you choose to provide, to answer questions and discuss projects. Your language and privacy choices are handled as explained in the Cookie policy. Please avoid sending identity documents, payment-card details or other sensitive personal information through chat.

3. Why we process information

Where the GDPR applies, our interests in operating a secure business website and responding to ordinary business enquiries provide the basis for those activities, subject to your rights and interests. Steps requested by an individual before a contract, and performance of a contract with that individual, may provide a contractual basis where applicable. Legal duties may require transaction records. Optional language storage and optional Tencent chat are enabled only with your consent; refusing them does not prevent browsing or emailing us.

4. Optional Tencent chat

The chat icon first offers information and a choice. No Tencent SDK is downloaded until you allow chat and activate it. Once enabled, Tencent / Knocket may handle messages, contact information, IP/device details, visitor or conversation identifiers, browser storage and service diagnostics. Our team may receive and reply to enquiries through Knocket and, if configured, its Telegram channel. The site restricts the separately identified Tencent analytics-script loader, but this does not mean the chat provider keeps no operational logs. See Tencent / Knocket privacy information. Email is an alternative that does not load the chat SDK.

5. Recipients and international access

Information may be accessible to authorised company staff, Cloudflare for hosting/security, Tencent / Knocket for chat you enable, and email or connected messaging providers used to answer your enquiry. Our team operates in Cambodia, and providers may process information outside your country, including outside the EEA. Their processing locations and retention depend on the services and account settings in use. Ask info@sscakh.com for the arrangements relevant to your enquiry and any applicable transfer safeguards. This notice does not state that a particular data-residency setting or transfer agreement has already been verified. See also the Cloudflare privacy policy.

6. How long information is kept

Privacy choices expire after 180 days. Optional language storage is removed when permission expires or is withdrawn. Business correspondence is kept while needed to respond, continue the business relationship, meet applicable recordkeeping duties or deal with a dispute; the relevant purpose and applicable legal obligations determine that period. Chat messages held by providers or in our inbox are separate from browser storage and follow the relevant service settings and business need. Contact us for the retention applicable to a specific enquiry. Clearing browser storage does not erase messages already delivered.

7. Your choices and rights

Use at any time to change optional choices. Turning off an active chat reloads the page to stop its running SDK, and removes identifiable chat-related storage accessible to this site; provider-held copies require a separate request. Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing and complain to a relevant supervisory authority. Email info@sscakh.com; we may ask for proportionate verification. Where the GDPR applies, we normally respond within one month, subject to lawful extensions.

8. Other information

This business website does not offer accounts or an online checkout, and is not directed at children. We do not use this website to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Links to maps, certification bodies and other external sites open only when selected; their own policies apply. Changes to this notice will appear here with a revised date.
