FACTORY FACTSSPRING SNOW TEXTILE · CAMBODIA

A clear view of our manufacturing.

Spring Snow Textile Co., Ltd. operates two knitwear factories in Cambodia. CHUN XUE, established in 2015, has 416 knitting machines, 1,000 employees and annual capacity of 4,000,000 pieces. SSCA, opened in 2026 for mid-to-high-end knitwear, has 100 knitting machines, 400 employees and annual capacity of 1,000,000 pieces.
